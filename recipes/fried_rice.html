Fried Rice

Home

Description

Frice rice is a globally loved, versatile dish made by stir-frying cooked rice in a wok or skillet with eggs, vegetables, and a variety of proteins. Seasoned with soy sauce and aromatics like garlic and ginger, it delivers a savory, smoky flavor and a perfectly firm texture.

Ingredients

  • Rice: Preferably cold, day-old long-grain or Jasmine rice.
  • Proteins: Beaten eggs, diced chicken, shrimp, beef, or pork.
  • Vegetables: Diced carrots, green peas, sweet corn, and chopped green onions.
  • Aromatics: Finely minced garlic and fresh ginger.
  • Sauces & Oils: Soy sauce (light and dark), sesame oil, and vegetable oil for frying.
  • Seasonings: White or black pepper, a pinch of sugar, and salt to taste.

Steps

  1. Heat vegetable oil in a large wok or skillet over high heat.
  2. Scramble the beaten eggs quickly in the pan until softly set, then remove and set them aside.
  3. Stir-fry your proteins (chicken, shrimp, or pork) until completely cooked through, then remove from the pan.
  4. Sauté the minced garlic and ginger in the remaining oil for about 30 seconds until highly fragrant.
  5. Toss in the dense vegetables like carrots, corn, and peas, stir-frying for 2 minutes until tender-crisp.
  6. Add the cold cooked rice to the pan, breaking up any large clumps with the back of your spatula.
  7. Drizzle the soy sauce and sesame oil evenly over the rice, stir-frying constantly on high heat.
  8. Season the mixture with salt, pepper, and a pinch of sugar, tossing rapidly to evenly coat the grains.
  9. Return the cooked proteins and scrambled eggs back into the pan, mixing them thoroughly with the rice.
  10. Garnish with chopped green onions and give the dish a final toss before removing it from the heat.
