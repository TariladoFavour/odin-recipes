Egusi Soup

Home

Description

Egusi soup is a classic West African culinary masterpeice crafted from ground shelled melon seeds, which give the dish its signature creamy texture and nutty undertone. Simmered in rich red palm oil alongside blended chili peppers and aromatic locust beans. It delivers a deeply savory flavour profile known as the ultimate comfort food. Packed with the assortments of succulent meats, fish, and vibrant green vegetables, it is traditionally enjoyed piping hot swallows like pounded yam or fufu.

Ingredients

  • Ground egusi seeds: 3 to 4 cups of finely milled melon seeds.
  • Red palm oil: 1 to 2 large cooking spoons.
  • Water or meat stock: For consistency and boiling.
  • Scotch bonnet peppers (Ata rodo): For heat.
  • Red bell peppers (Tatashe): For flavor and colour
  • Onions: Split between blending and sautéing.
  • Assorted Meats: Beef, goat meat, cow skin (ponmo), or tripe (shaki).
  • Fish: Smoked fish and dried stockfish head.
  • Ground Crayfish: For savory seafood undertones.
  • Fermented Locust Beans (Iru): For traditional umami flavor.
  • Seasoning Cubes & Salt: To taste.
  • Leafy Greens: Sliced Ugu (pumpkin leaves), bitter leaf, or spinach.

Steps

  1. Blend the pepper mix containing scotch bonnets, red bell peppers, and one onion until smooth.
  2. Mix the ground egusi seeds with a small amount of warm water or stock to form a thick paste.
  3. Boil your assorted meats, stockfish, and smoked fish with onions, seasoning cubes, and salt until tender.
  4. Heat the palm oil in a separate large pot over medium heat for 2 minutes.
  5. Sauté the chopped onions and fermented locust beans (iru) in the hot oil until fragrant.
  6. Fry the blended pepper mix in the oil for 10 to 15 minutes until the moisture evaporates.
  7. Scoop the egusi paste into the frying pepper sauce in small chunks, letting them set before gently stirring.
  8. Pour in the cooked meats, fish, meat stock, and ground crayfish, then stir carefully
  9. Simmer the mixture covered on medium-low heat for 15 minutes until the oil rises to the top.
  10. Stir in the chopped leafy greens and let the soup simmer uncovered for an additional 3 to 5 minutes.
